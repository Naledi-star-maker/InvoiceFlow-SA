# InvoiceFlow SA 💌

*Making small business invoicing and bookkeeping a breeze.*

A desktop business management app built for South African small businesses: garden services, salons, electricians, freelancers and more. It runs completely offline, with no cloud, no login and no subscriptions.

Built as a portfolio project, inspired by my mom's garden landscaping business.

> **Note:** This is a personal portfolio project. The source code is proprietary and is shown for viewing purposes only. See [License](#-license).

## 📋 Overview

Small business owners in South Africa often rely on manual, time-consuming methods like Word documents, handwritten notes or spreadsheets. InvoiceFlow SA replaces that with one simple desktop app: set up your business details once, add customers, create professional PDF invoices, track expenses and pay your staff.

## ✨ Features

- **Company Setup:** business and banking details entered once and reused everywhere
- **Customer Management:** add, edit, search and delete customers
- **Invoice Generator:** unlimited line items with live totals and automatic year-based invoice numbers
- **Manage Invoices:** reuse past invoices for month-end resends with a fresh number and "pay by" date
- **Professional PDF Invoices:** clean, itemised invoices with banking details, saved automatically
- **Dashboard and Reports:** monthly revenue, charts and recent activity
- **Budget & Expenses:** monthly budgets per category, expense tracking, spending charts, "things to improve" insights and a downloadable monthly accounting book
- **Employees and Payslips:** employee records, monthly hours-based pay, UIF calculation and South African payslip PDFs
- **Offline-First:** all data stays on your computer in a local database
